# Example Institute student handbook — fictional teaching data

This document is invented for the assignment. It is not an actual institution's policy.

## FEES-01 | Tuition fees and balances
Students can view their own assessed tuition, payments, outstanding balance and due date
through student services. Scholarship amounts are reported separately: do not subtract an
award from the outstanding balance unless a posted payment or credit records it.
For a dispute, create a finance support request. Do not invent a fee waiver.

## PAYMENT-02 | Payment portal failure and next working day
If the payment portal fails on the due date, a student may pay on the next working day
without a late charge ONLY if they report the failure to Finance on the original due date
and attach a timestamped screenshot or transaction reference to the official support ticket.
Working days are Monday through Friday excluding institute holidays. The agent has no
institute holiday calendar and must not guess the exact next working date. If the report
was late or supporting evidence is missing, Finance must decide; an extension is not guaranteed.
The chat demo can create a ticket but does not upload attachments: submit evidence through Finance.

## SCHOLARSHIP-03 | Scholarship eligibility and review
Merit and need-based scholarships are reviewed by the scholarship office. Students may
check their own recorded status. An approved award is not proof of payment or a fee credit.
Applications require the official form and supporting documents through student services.
No eligibility threshold or guaranteed processing date is specified in this sample handbook.

## COURSE-04 | Programme and course changes
Programme changes between PGDM-AI and PGDM-BDA require a review of academic prerequisites
and capacity by the academic office. A student must explicitly confirm their requested
programme before a change is recorded. In this fictional demonstration, confirmation
updates the sample programme record only; it does not represent real academic authorization.
The agent must explain the pending status and cannot confirm on a student's behalf.

## SUPPORT-05 | Support requests and escalation
Students can open their own finance, scholarship, academic or general support requests.
Include a brief description without passwords, one-time codes, bank account or card details.
The support office normally responds within two working days; this is a target, not a guarantee.
If the handbook does not address a question, state the gap and offer a general support request.

## PRIVACY-06 | Privacy and remembered preferences
Each student can access only their own private records, requests, memory, traces and approvals.
Remember only explicitly requested programme, response language or response style preferences.
Never remember a password, OTP, bank/card details, health details, or temporary circumstances.
Students can review or delete remembered facts at any time. Conversations are saved to the
student's account, can be read by authorised support staff, and are deleted by the student or
after the retention period. Messages containing secrets are withheld and never saved.

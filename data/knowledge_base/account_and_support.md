# Account, calendar and escalation — fictional teaching data

## EMAIL-14 | Updating your registered email
Students can ask Yukti to change the email address on their record and must give the new
address. The request is raised as a support ticket and the record changes only after support
staff approve it in the admin panel, normally within two working days; requests not decided in
seven days expire. The new address must not already belong to another student. If staff decline
the request, the ticket shows the reason and the record is unchanged.

## WORKDAY-15 | Working days definition
Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding institute holidays published by the academic office.
Weekends and institute holidays are not working days. Yukti has no holiday calendar and
cannot name an exact future working date; check the published academic calendar.

## ESCALATE-16 | Escalating an unanswered support request
Support staff aim to respond within two working days (SUPPORT-05). If a request has no staff
update after two working days, add a comment on the ticket asking for escalation; staff then
raise its priority. If it is still unresolved after five working days, the head of the relevant
office reviews it. Urgent finance issues on a due date should be marked high priority.

# Scholarship criteria — fictional teaching data

## MERIT-09 | Merit scholarship criteria
Merit scholarships are awarded each term to students in the top ten ranks of their programme
cohort with a term grade point average of at least 3.5 out of 4.0 and no disciplinary record.
Students must keep at least 75 per cent attendance in every course during the award term.
The award amount is set by the scholarship office and is shown on the student's record.

## CREDIT-10 | When an award is credited
An approved award is credited against tuition within ten working days of approval and only
after Finance posts it. Until it is posted, the full outstanding balance remains payable by the
due date. Students can ask about a missing credit through a scholarship support request.

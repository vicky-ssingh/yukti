# Fees and payments guide — fictional teaching data

## LATE-07 | Late payment penalties
A late payment charge of INR 500 applies for each working day after the due date that a
balance remains unpaid, up to a maximum of INR 5,000 per term. The charge does not apply
when the PAYMENT-02 portal-failure conditions are met. Finance may waive the charge only
through a finance support request; the agent cannot waive or promise a waiver.
Unpaid balances 30 calendar days after the due date place a hold on examination registration.

## INSTALMENT-08 | Paying tuition in instalments
A student may ask to split the outstanding tuition balance into two instalments. The request
must be made through a finance support request at least five working days before the due date.
Finance decides each request; it is not approved automatically. Approved plans pay half by the
original due date and the remainder within 30 calendar days. Late payment charges apply to a
missed instalment.

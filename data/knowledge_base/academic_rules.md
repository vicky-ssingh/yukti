# Academic rules — fictional teaching data

## ATTEND-11 | Attendance requirement
Students must attend at least 75 per cent of the scheduled sessions in each course.
Attendance is counted per course, not as an average across courses. A student below the
requirement is not eligible to sit the end-term examination for that course.

## CONDONE-12 | Attendance shortfall review
A student between 65 and 75 per cent may ask the academic office for a shortfall review through
an academic support request, with supporting documents submitted to the academic office.
Below 65 per cent the course must be repeated. The agent cannot approve a shortfall review.

## ELECTIVE-13 | Elective change window
Electives can be changed during the first ten working days of a term, subject to seat
availability, through an academic support request. Programme changes follow COURSE-04.

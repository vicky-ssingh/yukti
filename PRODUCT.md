# Yukti

A local coursework demonstration for the two Agentic AI Engineering assignments.
Students ask about fees, scholarships and policies in saved conversations with cited sources and
suggested follow-ups, raise and track tickets, save useful preferences, and confirm or reject
sensitive programme or email changes. Staff manage the knowledge base, tickets, users, roles,
settings and activity log in the admin panel.
The same Flask app demonstrates both assignments; Assignment 2 extends Assignment 1.
All records and policies are fictional and clearly labelled. Groq is the only live LLM provider.

Students and an evaluator use a laptop in a bright classroom, reading answers and
evidence side by side. A light surface and dark text support this setting.
Register: product. Restrained color strategy: warm paper surfaces, charcoal ink, one deep-green accent.
Priorities: understandable agent behavior, accurate evidence, visible approvals,
accessible forms, persistent safe preferences, and inspectable measurements.
